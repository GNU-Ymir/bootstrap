# E4309

> a union cannot be a member of another union

Raised during **validation**, from `ValidateErrorMessage::UNION_MEMBER_IN_UNION` (`src/ymirc/semantic/validator/errors.yr`).

## What it means

A union listed as the member of another union is neither flattened nor nested.
Flattening would make the member list of the outer union depend on a
declaration written elsewhere, and a match over it would have no member to
name, the inner union not being one of its cases.

## Example

`test_resources/type_union/test8.yr`:

```ymir
union U
| A
| B;

union V
| A
| U;
```

```text
Error[E4309] : a union cannot be a member of another union
 --> test_resources/type_union/test8.yr:(17,3)
17  ┃ | U;
    ╋   ^
 --> test_resources/type_union/test8.yr:(11,7)
11  ┃ union U
    ╋       -
    ┃
    = when validating test8::V -- (test_resources/type_union/test8.yr:(15,7))
    ┗━━━━━━━
```

## How to fix it

List the members of the inner union directly.
