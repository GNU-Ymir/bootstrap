# E4129

> cannot copy an expand value, maybe dcopy and expand keywords are inverted? (expand dcopy V)

Raised during **validation**, from `ValidateErrorMessage::MISMATCH_DCOPY_EXPAND` (`src/ymirc/semantic/validator/errors.yr`).

## What it means

`dcopy` was applied to an `expand` value; the keywords are inverted.

## How to fix it

Swap them: `expand dcopy v`.
