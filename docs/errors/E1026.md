# E1026

> unclosed parenthesis in version condition

Raised during **lexing**, from `LexingErrorMessage::UNCLOSED_VERSION_PAREN` (`src/ymirc/lexing/errors.yr`).

## What it means

A parenthesis opened in the condition of a `#if` or `#else if` directive is not
closed before the end of the line. The condition is read up to the end of the
line of its directive, so a parenthesis cannot be closed on the next one.

## Example

`test_resources/lexing/test43.yr`:

```ymir
#if (A | B
foo
#end
```

```text
Error[E1026] : unclosed parenthesis in version condition
 --> main.yr:(1,5)
 1  ┃ #if (A | B
    ╋     ^
    ┗━━━━━━━
```

## How to fix it

Close the parenthesis on the line of the directive, as in `#if (A | B) & C`.
