# E4130

> cannot copy a lazy value, maybe dcopy and lazy keywords are inverted? (lazy dcopy V)

Raised during **validation**, from `ValidateErrorMessage::MISMATCH_DCOPY_LAZY` (`src/ymirc/semantic/validator/errors.yr`).

## What it means

`dcopy` was applied to a `lazy` value; the keywords are inverted.

## How to fix it

Swap them: `lazy dcopy v`.
