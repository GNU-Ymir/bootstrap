# E4117

> malformed float literal <>

where `<>` stands for a value the diagnostic fills in.

Raised during **validation**, from `ValidateErrorMessage::MALFORMED_FLOAT_LITERAL` (`src/ymirc/semantic/validator/errors.yr`).

## What it means

A floating-point literal cannot be represented: it is malformed, or outside
what its type can hold.

## How to fix it

Correct the literal, or widen the type.
