# E4145

> nothing to catch

Raised during **validation**, from `ValidateErrorMessage::NOTHING_TO_CATCH` (`src/ymirc/semantic/validator/errors.yr`).

## What it means

A `catch` was written on a block that cannot throw anything.

## How to fix it

Remove the catch. If a call inside was expected to throw, check its
prototype: it may not declare the exception, or an inner catch may already
handle it.
