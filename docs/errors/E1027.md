# E1027

> unmatched closing parenthesis in version condition

Raised during **lexing**, from `LexingErrorMessage::UNMATCHED_VERSION_PAREN` (`src/ymirc/lexing/errors.yr`).

## What it means

The condition of a `#if` or `#else if` directive is followed by a closing
parenthesis that no opening one matches.

## Example

`test_resources/lexing/test44.yr`:

```ymir
#if A | B)
foo
#end
```

```text
Error[E1027] : unmatched closing parenthesis in version condition
 --> main.yr:(1,10)
 1  ┃ #if A | B)
    ╋          ^
    ┗━━━━━━━
```

## How to fix it

Remove the parenthesis, or open the group it was meant to close, as in
`#if (A | B)`.
