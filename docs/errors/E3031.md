# E3031

> unexpected declaration inside a macro

Raised during **declaration**, from `DeclareErrorMessage::UNEXPECTED_IN_MACRO` (`src/ymirc/semantic/declarator/errors.yr`).

## What it means

A declaration appeared inside a macro that a macro body does not accept.

## How to fix it

Move it out of the macro. A macro body holds rules and constructors, not
ordinary declarations.
