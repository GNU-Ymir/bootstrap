# E3033

> unexpected declaration inside a trait

Raised during **declaration**, from `DeclareErrorMessage::UNEXPECTED_IN_TRAIT` (`src/ymirc/semantic/declarator/errors.yr`).

## What it means

A declaration appeared inside a trait that a trait body does not accept. A
trait declares methods, with or without a default body.

## How to fix it

Move the declaration out of the trait. A trait cannot hold fields: the state a
method needs belongs to the type implementing it.
