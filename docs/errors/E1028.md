# E1028

> unexpected <> in version condition; a directive ends at the end of its line

Raised during **lexing**, from `LexingErrorMessage::UNEXPECTED_VERSION_TOKEN` (`src/ymirc/lexing/errors.yr`).

## What it means

The condition of a `#if` or `#else if` directive is read up to the end of its
line, and a token on that line is neither a version name, an operator (`|`,
`&`, `!`) nor a parenthesis where one can stand. Nothing but spaces can follow
the condition, not even code.

## Example

`test_resources/lexing/test47.yr`:

```ymir
#if A B
foo
#end
```

```text
Error[E1028] : unexpected B in version condition; a directive ends at the end of its line
 --> main.yr:(1,7)
 1  ┃ #if A B
    ╋       ^
    ┗━━━━━━━
```

## How to fix it

Combine the names with an operator, as in `#if A | B` (the operators are a
single `|` and `&`, not `||` and `&&`), and move any code to the next line.
