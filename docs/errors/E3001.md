# E3001

> a class cannot be both abstract and final

Raised during **declaration**, from `DeclareErrorMessage::ABSTRACT_AND_FINAL` (`src/ymirc/semantic/declarator/errors.yr`).

## What it means

A class is declared both `abstract` and `final`. `abstract` means the class
cannot be instantiated and must be derived from; `final` means it cannot be
derived from. Together they describe a class nothing can ever do anything
with.

## How to fix it

Drop whichever of the two does not apply.
