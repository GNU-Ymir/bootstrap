# E4009

> continue statement is not within a loop

Raised during **validation**, from `ValidateErrorMessage::CONTINUE_NO_LOOP` (`src/ymirc/semantic/validator/errors.yr`).

## What it means

A `continue` was written outside any loop.

## How to fix it

Remove it, or move it inside the loop it was meant to restart.
