# E4204

> the test of the template specialization failed

Raised during **validation**, from `ValidateErrorMessage::TEMPLATE_TEST_FAILED` (`src/ymirc/semantic/validator/errors.yr`).

## What it means

The `if` test attached to a template specialization evaluated to false, so the
specialization does not apply.

## How to fix it

Satisfy the test, or provide another specialization that covers the case.
