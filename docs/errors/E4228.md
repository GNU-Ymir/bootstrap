# E4228

> macro <> has no accessible constructors in that context

where `<>` stands for a value the diagnostic fills in.

Raised during **validation**, from `ValidateErrorMessage::UNDEFINED_CTOR_MACRO` (`src/ymirc/semantic/validator/errors.yr`).

## What it means

A macro was constructed and none of its constructors is accessible from
here.

## How to fix it

Check the protection of the macro constructors, or construct it from a module
that may see them.
