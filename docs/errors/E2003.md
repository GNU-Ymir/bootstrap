# E2003

> multiple constructor redirects are not permitted

Raised during **parsing**, from `SyntaxErrorMessage::MULTIPLE_AUX_CSTRS` (`src/ymirc/syntax/errors.yr`).

## What it means

A constructor redirects to another constructor more than once. A redirection
(`self (...)` or `super (...)` in the `with` clause) chooses which constructor
runs before this one, and only one can.

## How to fix it

Keep a single redirection. Move whatever the second one was initialising into
the constructor body, or into the constructor being redirected to.
